
    **Step 2: Wire cache into /transfer-list (src/bot/commands/transfers.py)**

    In the `transfer_list` function, after resolving `sport` and before calling `bot.transfer_store.list_players()`:

    Build a cache key: `f"transfer:{sport}"` if no position filter, or `f"transfer:{sport}:{position.lower()}"` if position is provided.

    Check `bot.transfer_cache.get(cache_key)`. If cache hit (not None), use the cached player list. If cache miss, call `bot.transfer_store.list_players(sport, position=position)` and then `bot.transfer_cache.set(cache_key, players)`.

    **Step 3: Invalidate cache on mutations (src/bot/commands/transfers.py)**

    In `transfer_add`, after a successful `bot.transfer_store.add_player()` call (when `player is not None`), invalidate all cached entries for that sport. Since we cannot iterate TTLCache keys, use a simple approach: call `bot.transfer_cache.clear()`. This is acceptable because:
    - Single-server bot with low traffic
    - Mutations are infrequent (editors only)
    - Over-invalidation is safe; under-invalidation would show stale data

    Similarly in `transfer_remove`, after a successful removal (`removed` is truthy), call `bot.transfer_cache.clear()`.

    - Mutation invalidates cache (list_players called again after add/remove)
